Last updated: 28 November 2026
The short versionKeep your password and PIN to yourself, use MADD only for lawful payments, and check who you're paying. Unusual transfers wait for your OK. If something isn't you, freeze your account and contact us.

1. About MADD

MADD is an Android e-wallet created as a Final Year Project at Asia Pacific University (APU), Malaysia. By creating an account or using the app you agree to these terms and to our privacy policy.

2. Your account

  • You must be 18 or older and give true details: your own name, email and Malaysian mobile number.
  • One person, one account. Don't create accounts for other people or share yours.
  • Keep your password and 6-digit PIN secret. We will never ask for them by email, phone or chat.
  • Tell us at once if you think someone else is using your account, and use "Freeze my account" in the app.

3. Sending and receiving money

  • Every transfer needs your PIN. Check the name shown before you confirm.
  • Sending money between MADD users has no fees.
  • A sent transfer can't be taken back by us. If you paid the wrong person, contact them or us.
  • You can only send what is in your balance.

4. Paused transfers and protection

To protect you, MADD may pause a transfer that looks unusual, for example a much bigger amount than usual, many transfers very quickly, or a transfer from a phone you haven't approved. While it is paused no money moves.

  • You have 5 minutes to release it with your PIN and a 6-digit code we email you. If you do nothing, it is cancelled.
  • Saying "No" freezes your account and signs out every phone.
  • Too many wrong passwords, PINs or codes pause access for a while.

5. Freezing and closing accounts

  • You can freeze your own account at any time. Only our support team can unfreeze it, after checking it's you.
  • We may freeze or close an account to protect you or others, for example if we suspect fraud or misuse. Every such action is logged with a written reason.
  • An account can only be closed when its balance is RM 0.00. Closed accounts can't sign in or receive money, and their records are kept.

6. What you must not do

  • Use MADD for anything illegal, including fraud, scams or money laundering.
  • Try to break, test or get around MADD's security without our written permission.
  • Pretend to be someone else or use another person's account.

7. Availability and liability

MADD is provided "as is". We work hard to keep it safe and running, but we can't promise it will always be available or free of errors. As far as the law allows, we aren't responsible for losses caused by you sharing your password or PIN, or by events outside our control.

8. Changes

We may update these terms. We will change the date at the top and tell you about important changes in the app or by email. If you keep using MADD after that, you accept the new terms.
