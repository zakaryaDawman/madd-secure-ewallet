FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is MADD free?

Yes. Sending and receiving money between MADD users has no fees.

What happens when a transfer is paused?

No money moves. You see why it was paused and have 5 minutes to confirm with your PIN and an emailed code, or say No, which freezes your account and signs out every phone. If you do nothing, it's cancelled.

I logged in on a new phone. Can I still send money?

You can sign in and look around, but transfers from the new phone wait until your usual phone approves it. This stops someone who stole your password from sending your money.

How do I unfreeze my account?

For your safety only our support team can unfreeze an account, after checking it's really you. Email maddwallet622@gmail.com.

What do you do with my data?

Only what's needed to run your wallet and protect it. We never sell it, and your password and PIN are stored scrambled so nobody can read them. Read our privacy policy.

Which phones does MADD work on?

MADD is available for Android phones.
