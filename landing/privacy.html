1. Who we are

MADD is a secure Android e-wallet built as a Final Year Project at Asia Pacific University of Technology & Innovation (APU), Malaysia. In this policy "MADD", "we" and "us" mean the MADD team. We handle personal data in line with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

2. What we collect

DataDetails
Account detailsYour full name, email address and Malaysian mobile number, given when you sign up (or by our team when they invite you).
Password and PINStored only as a one-way bcrypt hash. We can't see or recover them.
Wallet and transfersYour balance, and each transfer's amount, note, the person on the other side, time and status (sent, paused, cancelled, expired).
Device informationA random ID created by the app on your phone, the phone's name (for example "Honor REA-NX9"), and whether you trust it. Used to spot sign-ins from new phones.
Sign-in and PIN attemptsThe time and result of each sign-in and PIN check, so we can pause sign-in or transfers after too many wrong tries.
Security logA record of every paused transfer, freeze and important account change, with the reason.
Email codesOne-time 6-digit codes, stored only as a hash and valid for 5 minutes.
Push notification tokenAn address from your phone that lets us send alerts to your trusted phones.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos or anything from other apps.

3. Why we use it

  • To run your wallet: create your account, sign you in, show your balance and move money.
  • To protect you from fraud: check every transfer before it leaves, pause unusual ones, and ask you first.
  • To contact you: send sign-up codes, codes to release a paused transfer, and alerts such as "new phone signed in" or "phone number changed".
  • To help you: answer support requests and let our team freeze an account to protect it.

We don't use your data for advertising and we don't build marketing profiles.

4. Who we share it with

We never sell your personal data. We only share what is needed with these services, which work for us:

  • Google (Gmail): to send our emails to your address.
  • Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging): to deliver push alerts to your trusted phones.

When you send money, the other person sees a partly hidden version of your name and number (for example "Ahm*** Ali", "+60 12-*** 4521"). We may share data if the law requires it.

5. How we protect it

  • Passwords, PINs and email codes are stored as bcrypt hashes, never as plain text.
  • Every security decision is made on our server, not on your phone.
  • Sessions end after 30 minutes, when you log out, or when your account is frozen.
  • Too many wrong passwords, PINs or codes pause access for a while.
  • The security log can't be edited or deleted, not even by our own code.
  • Our support team can freeze or unfreeze an account, always with a written reason that is logged, but can never move your money, change your balance or see your PIN.
  • Receipts and QR codes only show your phone number and a partly hidden name, never your balance.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account is open. Transfers and the security log are kept as a permanent record, because the other person in a transfer needs a correct history too, and records of money movements must stay accurate. If your account is closed, it is deactivated: you can't sign in or be paid, but these records are kept and are not deleted. Email codes stop working after 5 minutes.

7. Your rights

Under the PDPA you can ask us to:

  • see the personal data we hold about you,
  • correct details that are wrong (you can change your name and phone number in the app yourself),
  • close your account,
  • stop sending you messages that aren't needed to run or protect your account.

Email maddwallet622@gmail.com. We may ask you to prove it's you first.

8. Phone permissions

  • Camera: only to scan MADD QR codes. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.
  • Photos: only when you choose a picture to scan a QR code from, or save your QR or a receipt to your gallery.
  • Notifications: to alert you about paused transfers, new sign-ins and money received.

You can turn these off in your phone's settings at any time.

9. This website

This website has no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. It is hosted on Netlify, which may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect the site.

10. Children

MADD is not meant for children under 18. If you think a child has given us their data, contact us and we will close the account.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. For important changes we will tell you in the app or by email.
